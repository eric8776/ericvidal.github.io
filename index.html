Frequently Asked Questions

Is the treatment safe for my pets and kids?

Absolutely. We apply precision perimeter treatments to exterior target zones. Once our low-impact barriers dry completely (typically within 30 minutes), your yard is entirely open and completely safe for children and pets to play on.

What happens if it rains right after a service?

Modern materials are completely water-insoluble once dry, meaning seasonal rain won't wash away your barrier protective shield. If unseasonal downpours break your barrier early, our subscription guarantee covers immediate re-treatment completely free of charge.

California Pesticide Disclosure Notice

Pursuant to California Business & Professions Code Section 8538

NOTICE TO OWNER / OCCUPANT:

CAUTION — PESTICIDES ARE TOXIC CHEMICALS. Structural Pest Control Companies are licensed and regulated by the Structural Pest Control Board, and can apply pesticides for the control of structural pests or organisms only after registration of the chemicals with the Department of Pesticide Regulation.

Registration is granted when the Department finds that the chemical will control the pest and will not cause unreasonable adverse effects to humans or the environment when used in accordance with label directions. The Department of Pesticide Regulation and the Department of Health Services utilize scientists and toxicologists to review data submitted by chemical manufacturers prior to registration.

Common Materials Approved & Proposed for Defense Use:

  • Bifenthrin / Deltamethrin: Target Pests: Ants, Spiders, Roaches, Earwigs, Silverfish, Crickets.
